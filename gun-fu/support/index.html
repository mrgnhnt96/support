Stickman Gun-Fu

Email mrgnhnt96@gmail.com. Include your device and OS version and we will get back to you.

I bought something and it did not appear

Open Armory and tap Restore Purchases. That re-checks your App Store or Google Play account and re-applies anything you own. If it still does not show up, email us with the receipt and we will sort it out.

I changed phones and lost my progress

Progress — coins, stats, upgrades — is stored on the device, so it does not move with you. Purchases do: use Restore Purchases on the new phone.

How do I get rid of the ads?

Remove Ads in the Armory removes the interstitial between games. The rewarded ad that revives you stays, because it is always your choice to watch it.

There is no sound

Check Settings in the game — music and sound effects have separate sliders, and haptics have their own switch. On iPhone, also check the silent switch: the game respects it deliberately, so it will not talk over you in a meeting.

The game is too hard

That is Epic. Try Normal, and spend coins on Shield for extra health and Focus to buy yourself more time before enemies fire.

I want a refund

Refunds are handled by the store, not by us. On iOS use reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android use the Google Play order history. If the store turns you down and you think it was our fault, email us.

I found a bug

Please tell us. What you were doing, what happened, your device and OS version, and a screenshot if you have one.
