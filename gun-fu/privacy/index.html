Privacy Policy

Stickman Gun-Fu · Last updated 14 August 2026

Stickman Gun-Fu is made by MRGNHNT, LLC. There is no account to create, no sign-up, and no login. We do not ask you for your name, your email address, or anything else about you, and we do not have a server that stores your data.

The game does use a small number of third-party services to show ads, take payments, and run leaderboards. Those services collect data under their own policies, and the rest of this page explains which ones and why.

What stays on your device

Your progress is saved on your phone and nowhere else:

  • Kills, games played, best scores and survival times
  • Coins, purchased weapons, and upgrade levels
  • Which achievements you have unlocked
  • Your music, sound effect and haptics settings

Deleting the app deletes all of it. We never see it.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: an interstitial between some games, and an optional rewarded ad you can choose to watch to revive. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to select and measure ads.

On iOS the game asks for permission before any tracking happens, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads still appear but are not personalised. In regions that require it, such as the EEA and UK, Google's consent form asks how your data may be used before ads are loaded.

Buying Remove Ads stops the interstitials. Rewarded ads remain available and remain optional.

Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card details. Purchases are managed through RevenueCat, which records an anonymous identifier and your purchase history so that what you have bought can be restored on a new device.

RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Leaderboards and achievements

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center or Google Play Games, the game submits your kill counts to leaderboards and reports achievements you unlock. That happens under your platform account, and Apple and Google handle it. If you are not signed in, the game still works and everything stays local.

Children

Stickman Gun-Fu is rated 12+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through one of the services above, email us and we will help you get it removed.

Your choices

  • iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking to turn tracking off
  • Android: Settings › Google › Ads to reset or delete your advertising ID
  • Delete the app to erase everything stored on your device

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
